LibertyClips

Short GTA 6 gameplay highlights, with credit to the original creator.

What the app does

LibertyClips is a private tool run by the owner of the LibertyClips channels. It prepares short vertical clips from GTA 6 videos and uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public, and nobody else can sign in to it.

Google account access

The app asks for one permission, upload YouTube videos (youtube.upload), and uses it only to upload clips to the owner's channel. Details are in the privacy policy.

Credit and removal

Each clip's description credits the creator and links to the original video. If you are a creator or rights holder and want a clip removed, write to support@hadid.fitness.

LibertyClips is a fan project. It is not affiliated with Rockstar Games, Take-Two Interactive, YouTube or Google.
